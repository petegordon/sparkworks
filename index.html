What's this?

It's improv comedy, but the bit is software. The audience throws prompts at us — features, themes, ridiculous constraints — and we build the app live, on stage, in 45 minutes.

No script. No safety net. No backup slides if the model rate-limits us.

The prompt: "Build a Ghost Hunter app with National Parks picnic planning for picky eaters, add the Three Body Problem, graffiti style."

Three frontier models took the same nonsense and shipped. Pick a fighter.
